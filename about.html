About

Geography, GIS & Cultural Resources

I’m Bryan Ramos-Morales, a Geography and Environmental Resources student at California State University, Stanislaus, developing work across GIS, environmental geography, cultural resources, spatial analysis, and public-facing communication.

How I Think

Geography is about relationships.

I’m most interested in what becomes visible when different layers of a place are considered together.

A river, road, boundary, wetland, neighborhood, volcanic zone, institution, or historic landscape may tell one story on its own. Geography becomes more useful when those systems are examined together and we ask why they overlap, where they differ, and what those patterns mean.

What I Work On

Environmental & spatial questions

My projects often begin with a geographic question and develop through research, data organization, GIS analysis, cartographic design, and written interpretation.

My current work includes environmental systems, historic landscapes, flood risk, volcanism and glaciation, cultural geography, NAGPRA/CalNAGPRA communication, and statewide spatial patterns.
