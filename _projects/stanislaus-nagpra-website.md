## Project Overview

I created and continue to help maintain the NAGPRA and CalNAGPRA section of the Stanislaus State website through the Office of the President.

The website section serves as a centralized public resource where visitors can find information about NAGPRA and CalNAGPRA, campus contacts, published notices, policies, reporting resources, educational materials, and related university information.

Because this work involves sensitive cultural, legal, and institutional responsibilities, I focused on presenting the information clearly, respectfully, and in a way that visitors can navigate easily.

## My Role and Process

I helped build the university’s NAGPRA web presence from the ground up by developing the page structure, organizing information, creating a clear navigation system, placing content, and maintaining updates for the office.

My work included building the main landing page, organizing related pages into a consistent structure, updating published notices and public resources, and helping present sensitive information in a respectful and accessible format.

I continue to work closely with the NAGPRA/CalNAGPRA Coordinator as part of the small team responsible for maintaining the website section and supporting the university’s NAGPRA responsibilities.

## Information Architecture

The website needed to support several different audiences, including Tribes and Tribal affiliates, campus users, researchers, and members of the public.

I organized the section so visitors could move between educational information, official contacts, published notices, policies, inventories and summaries, reporting resources, and related university materials without having to search across disconnected pages.

The navigation and page hierarchy were designed to keep important information visible while allowing the section to expand as new notices, resources, and responsibilities are added.

## Impact

This project established a centralized online resource for Stanislaus State’s NAGPRA and CalNAGPRA information.

The website helps Tribes, Tribal affiliates, campus users, researchers, and members of the public locate official contacts, policies, notices, reporting information, and educational resources in one organized location.

Because the website section is actively maintained, it also supports the office’s ongoing communication responsibilities by providing a consistent place where new notices, resources, and updates can be published as the work continues.
