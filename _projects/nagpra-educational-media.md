## Project Overview

I created public-facing educational materials to support NAGPRA and CalNAGPRA communication at Stanislaus State.

The project included an informational brochure introducing NAGPRA, CalNAGPRA, university contacts, and related resources, as well as a promotional flyer for the Smudging Space Dedication Ceremony.

The goal was to make institutional information easier to understand while keeping the designs respectful, organized, visually clear, and connected to the purpose of each project.

## Informational Brochure

The brochure provides a portable introduction to NAGPRA and CalNAGPRA, explains Stanislaus State’s responsibilities, identifies university contacts, and directs readers to additional resources.

I organized the information across six panels so readers could move from introductory material to university-specific information, contacts, and resource access.

The visual system combines university colors, California landscape and plant imagery, cultural-resource imagery, and a QR code directing readers to additional information.

I used photographs of the campus setting, stone landscape, pine elements, and ceremonial imagery to create a strong visual connection to the location and event.

## Design Approach

For the brochure, I focused on educational clarity, resource access, and a visual sequence that guides readers naturally across the folded panels.

For the event flyer, I created a more promotional layout centered on the ceremony location, event details, and visual connection to the outdoor space.

Both materials required organizing written information, selecting and arranging images, refining visual hierarchy, and ensuring that the finished designs were appropriate for their intended audiences.

## My Role and Process

I developed the visual layouts, organized the written information, selected and arranged images, and refined the designs for public distribution.

I worked with the NAGPRA/CalNAGPRA Coordinator to review the materials and help ensure that the content and presentation were accurate, respectful, and appropriate for their intended uses.

The brochure focused on education and resource access, while the flyer focused on event communication. Creating both required adapting the design approach to two different communication goals while maintaining a consistent institutional connection.

## Impact

These materials supported public education, campus communication, and outreach related to NAGPRA, CalNAGPRA, and the Smudging Space Dedication.

The brochure provided a portable introduction to university responsibilities, contacts, and resources.

The flyer helped communicate the location, timing, and purpose of a significant campus event and supported public awareness of the Smudging Space dedication.
